Team Members of EZ Healthcare App : Jahnelle, Adrianna, Matthew and Rahul
Project Description : This application will serve a purchase in the healthcare industry,
in such a tech-savy world, it is difficult for our elderly community to assist themselves which
strips them of their independence and they have to completely rely on someone else to assist them.
This app is designed to give back the elderly their independence with an application that makes
everything so easy hence the name EZ.
